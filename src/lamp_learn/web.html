The lamp (lamp-sim)

Pick a lesson and press Start.

The voice plays from your Mac. The ring shows lamp-sim's 32 lights: green when it hears the phrase, orange on a miss.

You are the learner

—

Practice talk: answer freely, in full sentences. On a real lamp the lamp's AI talks back with you; the virtual lamp has no AI, so here you just practise speaking and see what Chrome heard. Not scored.

Answer after the lamp finishes its line: anything said while it speaks is ignored. A wrong line is not reported by the lamp (it only tells which of its phrases it heard), so a miss shows when the countdown ends. Your line goes to lamp-sim as if its recognizer heard it, and the lamp's own phrase matching decides.
