# English lesson

The learner's computer runs lamp-learn's listening mode: it hears "start my
English lesson" through the lamp's phrase matching and runs the lesson
through the lamp. This tells the lamp's own model the words are handled, so
it says a short "starting" line instead of answering them itself.
