# English Tutor

An English conversation partner for anyone learning English. Say "let's
practise English" (and a level or topic if you like: "at B1, about travel")
and the lamp talks with you in English: it answers what you say, asks a short
question back, and when something could be said better, says the better way
once, briefly, then carries on. It nods for a line said well and tilts its
head before a correction.

Works on its own. With lamp-learn (github.com/bos-hieu/lamp-learn) running on
a computer, "start my English lesson" also starts scored lessons.
